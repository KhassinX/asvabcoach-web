- Be inaccurate or incomplete
- Differ from the "official" explanation shown in the same question
- Reflect the limitations of a small on-device language model

Treat AI explanations as a **supplementary study tool**, not as authoritative. The hand-curated "official" explanation in each question is the ground truth. If an AI explanation seems wrong or contradicts the official explanation, trust the official one.

ASVAB Coach displays minimum AFQT score requirements per military branch (e.g., "Army: 31, Marines: 32, Space Force: 36"). These reflect **current DoD policy** as of the app's last update.

- DoD policy on minimum AFQT scores **changes periodically**. We update the app quarterly to track changes, but there may be a lag between a policy change and our update.
- The displayed minimums are **baseline requirements**. Specific jobs (MOS/rating) within each branch have their own additional score requirements (line scores) that may be higher.
- Always **verify current requirements with an official recruiter** of your chosen branch before making enlistment decisions based on app data.
